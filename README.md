# quantized_linear_probes

On Aug. 17, 2026 I was preparing to apply to AIAF Fellowship - I only had a day or two to prepare. Their application included a brief video discussing a critique or extension of one of their recent results, including a steering vector result. I remembered an incidental observation while I worked at Battelle - another team had prepared linear probes for misbehavior classification (or possibly control vectors?). They'd prepared these on full-bit and on quantized models, and looking at the plots I saw their probes were effective on earlier layers in the full-bit models than the quantized - e.g. a 2- or 3-layer delay when working on the quantized model. I commented, and the coworker was curious, and nothing came of it. Excited to apply for a fellowship, I wrote out the experiment and set it running. Turns out, I was wrong - linear probes following the Geometry-of-Truth paper's structure, trained on GPT-2-Small and Llama-2-13b have no distinguishable difference when trained on quantized vs unquantized models. With the deadline approaching, I decided to not apply: my LW post was still pending, my critique was wrong, and my preparations were underwhelming. I had dinner and went on a walk instead. No effort to craft a test which would strengthen my case; I simply ran the experiment which would convince me, and it convinced me.
